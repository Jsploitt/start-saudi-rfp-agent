Tracked separately — bank account and GM visa, continued

Bank account

Opens once your CR is active. Banks typically take 2–3 weeks and run their own compliance entirely outside the registration process — it can take as long as registration itself.

Opening the account after incorporation may need a visit depending on the bank, and we tell you upfront. You've told us neither director has been to Saudi Arabia and that extended travel is difficult for Katherine — worth planning around now, and we'll tell you which banks can proceed without one as soon as we know your structure.

GM visa and residency

The company needs a General Manager from day one. The GM does not have to be a hired manager — if you own the company, you can list yourself as GM.

Owning and residing are legally separate questions. You can appoint a GM who already lives in Saudi Arabia, or become the resident GM yourself, which requires the GM visa, medical checks and a move.

You've told us to treat Daniel Ayre's visa as a later engagement, once the work materialises, and to give you the cost now. We've priced it as an optional service below — it runs on its own timeline, separately from this scope.
