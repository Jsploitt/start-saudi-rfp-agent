What we need from you, and when, continued

Attestation, in plain terms

Attestation is the chain of approvals that makes a UK document recognised in Saudi Arabia. Because the UK is a Hague Apostille Convention member, your documents can be apostilled at home and then attested by Saudi authorities, without a separate Saudi embassy visit.

This sits before the 15-working-day clock, not inside it. You've confirmed you can start apostille on your FY2025 accounts now: do that today, alongside your certificate of incorporation and articles.

The last three years of filed accounts, plus your certificate of incorporation and articles, are what we need. Your 2026 accounts, due at the accountant by end of October, are not required to start.
