## Read this first

**Taajeel proposals are slide decks, not documents.** All three supplied are 16:9 landscape, 27–33 slides, with section dividers, infographic process diagrams and a signature page. Two were exported to PDF; one went out through DocuSign as an engagement letter. The Start Saudi adaptation keeps the deck and cuts it to 19–21 slides.

## The two findings that change the build

**1 · The parent company writes in the register `content/voice.md` bans.** Taajeel's About slide opens *"Taajeel stands as a beacon of excellence in the business solutions landscape, seamlessly merging innovation with expertise to empower organizations…"* — fifteen banned items across two short passages. `voice.md` was written from the Start Saudi site before these documents existed, and its "Before" examples turned out to be close paraphrases of the parent's real copy. **A model drafting in this space will drift toward that register, and the drift produces fluent, professional, entirely unobjectionable prose.** Fluency is the failure mode. See `voice.md` § 3a.

**2 · Taajeel's own timeline says 31 days where Start Saudi promises 15 working days.** For the same product — a foreign-owned company formation. Plus a further 47 days for the GM residency. This is now the highest-priority open question in the kit (**Q35**), and until it is answered no Start Saudi proposal can publish a per-step duration table, because the parent company's own client-facing chart contradicts it.

## What the proposals unblocked

Pricing structure and real figures · payment milestones · VAT treatment · the full GM residency path and its fees · the contracting entity and its CR number · the standard terms · the liability position (capped at fees paid, no service-level remedy) · the MISA document list · two government platforms the Start Saudi site never mentions (Muqeem, MUDAD).

Eleven open questions closed or narrowed. See `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
