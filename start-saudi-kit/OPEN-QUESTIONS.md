## Tier 1 — blocking. Send these now.

**Q35 · Why does Taajeel's own timeline say 31 days when Start Saudi promises 15 working days?** ⚠️ *(`content/04-process.md`, `proposal/structure.md`, `proposal/section-briefs.md`)*
**NEW, and now the most important question in the kit.** Taajeel's Time Frame slide for the Havenstone foreign company formation — the same product Start Saudi sells — sets out 16 steps totalling **31 days**, drawn sequentially, plus a further **47 days** for the GM residency. Start Saudi publishes **15 working days** (~21 calendar days) across 6 steps with two of them in parallel.

Is Start Saudi a genuinely different, leaner product? Is Taajeel's chart conservative, in calendar days, and unable to draw parallelism? Or are the two measuring different spans — the failure Start Saudi's own blog warns about? Until this is settled, no Start Saudi proposal can safely publish a per-step duration table, because the parent company's own client-facing chart contradicts it.

**Q17 · What does Start Saudi charge?** *(`content/05-pricing.md`)*
**Substantially narrowed.** Taajeel's pricing is now known: **SAR 40,000** for the Havenstone foreign company formation plus GM resident visa, VAT SAR 6,000, milestones **50 / 35 / 15**, and a full optional price list with units. Government fees are explicitly **excluded** from Taajeel's fee, which answers the markup question structurally.

What remains: **is the Start Saudi product priced differently from Taajeel's bespoke engagements?** A standardised fast track would normally be cheaper. SAR 40,000 is a calibration point, not an answer, and a proposal cannot quote it.

**Q1 · Can we have the brand guidelines in a readable form?** *(`brand/typography.md`, `brand/tokens.json`)*
`startsaudi brand guidelines.pdf` is a 76 MB flattened image-only export; it returns an empty text layer and its bytes are not retrievable from this environment. **Attaching it directly to a session, or just the palette / typography / logo-usage pages as images, closes this.** Unknowns: the official hex values (measured from the PNGs, unverified); **the typeface, which we could not identify** — of twenty-one candidate faces tested, the top ten scored within 0.06 of each other, the signature of no match; and **whether an Arabic typeface is specified, and which.**

**Q13 · The ChiefNest story, with dates and an outcome.** *(`content/03-credentials.md`)*
Dr. Sulaiman Alsalameh is a partner in Start Saudi, so this is available for the asking. What does ChiefNest do, what was registered, when, how long did it take, and what were the *"few places"* he went first that cost him money? **One completed case study with real dates would be worth more in a proposal than all four headline track-record numbers**, and it is the cheapest item on this list to obtain.

**Q8 · Which entity does a Start Saudi proposal contract as?** *(`content/01-company.md`, `proposal/section-briefs.md`)*
**Narrowed.** Taajeel contracts as **Taajeel Business Solutions Co. LLC** (CR 1010941670) and its General Manager signs. The question is now narrowly whether a Start Saudi proposal contracts under that same entity, or whether Start Saudi is a separate contracting party. A proposal names a signatory; this cannot stay open.

**Q26 · Who selects the activity code, and who is accountable if it is wrong?** *(`content/06-faq.md`, `content/02-services/01-misa-investment-licence.md`)*
Activity classification determines ownership rules, capital thresholds and approvals — the SAR 30m trading threshold turns on it. Is classification advice part of the service? Sharpened by a real tension: Taajeel's standard Terms disclaim responsibility for *"legal, regulatory, accounting, Zakat, or taxation matters"*, which sits awkwardly beside a proposal that classifies a client's activity band.
