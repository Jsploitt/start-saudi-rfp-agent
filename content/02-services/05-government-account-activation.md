## What it is

**[VERBATIM]** — homepage: *"Tax, social insurance and labour portals switched on."*

**[SOURCED]** The platforms:

| Platform | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Qiwa** | Ministry of Human Resources labour platform — establishment file, employment contracts, visa requests |
| **GOSI** | General Organization for Social Insurance — employer file, contributions |
| **ZATCA** | Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority — the company's tax file; VAT registration where thresholds are met |
| **National Address** | The registered address, required by banks and by other registrations |
| **Chamber of Commerce** | Membership, renewed annually |

**[SOURCED — Taajeel's Havenstone proposal, June 2026.]** Taajeel's own 16-step formation path adds **two platforms the Start Saudi site never mentions**:

| Platform | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Muqeem** | Ministry of Interior residency platform — procedures for foreign residents. Carries a subscription (~SAR 1,265/yr) plus a points balance (~SAR 575 for 2,500 points) |
| **MUDAD** | Payroll submission platform, used for wage-protection compliance |

**[EXTRAPOLATED]** Neither is optional for a company that intends to employ anyone or to bring in a resident GM — Muqeem is where residency procedures run and MUDAD is where payroll is filed. Their absence from the Start Saudi six-step path is a genuine gap in the public description, and naming them in a proposal is exactly the kind of "here is what you did not know to ask about" move the brand is built on. See OPEN-QUESTIONS Q32.

## What Start Saudi does

**[SOURCED]** Activates the accounts and confirms they are live, within 24 hours of the CR.

**[SOURCED]** But note what the blog says about how these open:

> **[VERBATIM — blog]** "Your establishment file on Qiwa opens automatically when your CR is issued."

**[SOURCED]** GOSI, ZATCA, the National Address and the Chamber of Commerce registration likewise open automatically on CR issuance.

**[EXTRAPOLATED]** This creates a real and awkward ambiguity about what is being sold. If the files open automatically, the service is **configuration and verification** — completing the company profile, confirming the address details are right, checking each file is actually live and correctly set up — rather than causing them to exist. That is genuine work and it is worth paying for, but a proposal that implies Taajeel switches on something that would otherwise stay off is overstating it. **[TO CONFIRM: exactly what is done at this step beyond confirming automatic activation.]** See OPEN-QUESTIONS Q32.

## What each platform means in practice

**[SOURCED]**

**Qiwa.** Complete the company profile, then issue employment contracts and visa requests through it. **Necessary even with no employees yet** — and it is the platform the GM visa runs through, which makes it the bridge between registration and the post-incorporation work.

**GOSI.** The employer file opens automatically; actual registration happens when staff are hired. Contribution rates depend on employee nationality and on registration timing.

**ZATCA.** The tax file opens automatically. **VAT registration follows only if revenue exceeds the threshold.** A foreign founder will want to know whether they must register immediately — the answer depends on expected revenue and activity. *(Saudi VAT is 15% — general knowledge, not from any Start Saudi source. Verify the current rate and the registration threshold before either appears in a client document.)*

**National Address.** **[SOURCED]** Required by banks — which makes it a dependency of the bank account, not just an administrative record. **[SOURCED as cited, not verified]** Roughly SAR 200 per year; see the warning at the head of the fee table in `05-pricing.md` before using that figure, which is third-party guidance rather than a Taajeel or government quotation.

**Chamber of Commerce.** Annual subscription. **[SOURCED]** *"pay Chamber membership fees annually to maintain active status"*.

## What commonly goes wrong

**[EXTRAPOLATED]**
- **"Opened automatically" read as "configured and compliant".** The file existing is not the profile being complete.
- **National Address details wrong or unconfirmed**, discovered at the bank
- **Chamber subscription lapses**, and the registration goes inactive — the *"Is something up for renewal that nobody told me about?"* failure exactly
- **VAT registration assumed to be automatic.** It is not; it is threshold-driven
- **GOSI obligations misunderstood at first hire** — the employer file existing is not the same as employees being registered
